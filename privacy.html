Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

The PeakClips app ("we") is a private publishing tool operated by PeakClips. It is used only to upload videos to accounts owned by PeakClips.

Data we access

  • OAuth access/refresh tokens for our own YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts, used solely to upload videos and read upload status.
  • Basic account info returned by those platforms (account ID, username).

How data is used and stored

Tokens are stored on a private, access-restricted server, are never shared, sold or used for advertising, and are used only to perform uploads we initiate. We do not collect data about viewers or third-party users.

Google API Services

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube API use is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy.

Deletion

You can revoke access at any time from your Google, Meta or TikTok account settings. To request deletion of any stored data, email us; we delete it within 30 days.
